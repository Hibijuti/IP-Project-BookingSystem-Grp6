Frequently Asked.

Where do you deliver?
We deliver strictly across Lapu-Lapu City (Mactan Island), including Basak, Marigondon, Punta Engaño, Subabasbas, Agus, Gun-ob, Mactan, and nearby subdivisions and beach resorts.
When does my rental timer start?
Our team arrives 15–30 minutes before your requested time to set up the TV, stands, and soundcheck the JBL speaker and wireless mics. Your 12-hour or 22-hour rental timer only starts when everything is tested and ready to sing.
What songs are included with the Karaoke player?
Our TJ Maestro Karaoke player comes loaded with thousands of tracks — from timeless classic ballads and 90s/2000s OPM anthems to current pop hits.
How do I reserve my date?
Simply head to our Book Now page, select an available date on the calendar, pick your package, and tap Complete Reservation to message us directly on Facebook Messenger.
